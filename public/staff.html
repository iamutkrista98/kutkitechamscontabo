About this portal

Use the dial on your dashboard to check in each morning and check out at the end of your shift. If you need to leave before 18:00, the system will ask for a brief reason — this is sent straight to the HR office for approval, and you can track its status under Early Checkouts at any time.

Each check-in and check-out records your approximate location and area (if you allow the browser prompt) and the IP address of the device you used, so HR has an accurate picture of where and how the team is working.

The work week runs Sunday to Friday, with the rest as weekly off. Your Monthly Report updates automatically as you check in and out.
